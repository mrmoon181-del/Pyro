# pyro
🔥 PYRO – AI-Powered Cold Calling Assistant

Turning cold calls into intelligent conversations with AI.

PYRO is an AI-powered Cold Calling Assistant designed to make sales conversations smarter, more structured, and more effective. It provides sales representatives with an organized eight-stage calling workflow, prospect information, conversation guidance, suggested responses, and objection-handling assistance.

The goal is to simplify traditional cold calling and help sales professionals communicate with greater confidence, consistency, and efficiency.

---

🚀 Project Overview

Cold calling is an important part of sales, but sales representatives often struggle with conversation structure, handling objections, maintaining consistency, and managing prospect interactions.

PYRO addresses these challenges through an intelligent workspace that guides users throughout the calling process.

Instead of relying entirely on manual scripts, users can follow a structured workflow with contextual guidance and suggested responses.

✨ Key Features

- 8-Stage Calling Workflow: A structured process to guide representatives through different stages of a sales call.
- Prospect Management: Organized prospect details and relevant information.
- AI-Powered Assistance: Intelligent conversation guidance and response suggestions.
- Objection Handling: Assistance with handling common sales objections.
- Call Progress Tracking: Track progress throughout the calling workflow.
- Interactive Workspace: A focused interface for managing sales conversations.
- Conversation Guidance: Suggested talking points to improve communication and consistency.

🔄 How It Works

1. Opening & Disclosure – Start the conversation professionally and establish transparency.
2. Introduction – Introduce yourself and the purpose of the call.
3. Rapport Building – Establish a connection with the prospect.
4. Discovery – Understand the prospect's requirements and challenges.
5. Value Proposition – Communicate how the product or service can help.
6. Objection Handling – Address questions, concerns, and objections.
7. Closing – Guide the conversation towards a suitable next step.
8. Follow-Up – Record the outcome and plan further communication.
